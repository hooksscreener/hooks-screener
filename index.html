◈ About This Screener
Quality · Momentum · Edge
This screener was built around one idea: finding great businesses at the right price, at the right time. It doesn't just ask whether a stock looks good today. It asks whether the business is fundamentally strong, whether the market is starting to recognize that strength, whether the stock is currently offering a real discount, and whether the selling pressure has clearly stopped. All questions have to be answered simultaneously before the screener signals a buy.
The Core Idea
Fundamentals tell you why a stock deserves a position. Momentum tells you when to actually buy it.
"Is this a great business, in a real uptrend, currently on sale — and has the selling pressure just stopped?" That is the question this screener is designed to answer.
The Three Phases
Each stock must clear all three phases to qualify. Entry timing indicators then help you decide exactly when to act.
Phase 1
Value — Is This a Good Business?
Inspired by Warren Buffett. Looks only at the quality of the company — how efficiently it generates profit, how much real cash it produces, and how much debt it carries.
Phase 2
Trend — Is the Market Agreeing?
A great business can stay flat for years if the market hasn't recognized it. This phase confirms sustained price strength and a rising long-term average.
Phase 3
Dip — Is It Currently on Sale?
Confirms the stock has pulled back from its highs into a healthy range — creating a better entry point than buying after a major run-up. Ranked by how close to the optimal dip level.
Entry Timing
When Should You Actually Buy?
Advisory indicators — RSI, MACD, and volatility compression — show whether the entry timing is Early, Approaching, or Confirmed. Not a hard gate, but essential context.
Portfolio Philosophy
Concentration
15–20 positions. Concentrated enough that each pick matters.
Review cadence
Quarterly, timed around earnings season.
Holding period
8–24 months. Long enough for a thesis to play out.
When to sell
Three explicit triggers defined in advance — fundamental deterioration, leverage spike, or trend breakdown.
What This Is Not
Not a prediction system. Not financial advice. A disciplined framework for identifying businesses worth owning at moments when the market offers a reasonable entry.
For informational and research purposes only. Always conduct your own research before investing.
